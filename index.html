01 / Research

Selected work

My current focus is RL alignment of latent diffusion language models. My broader interests include protein design, Physics-informed ML, and evolutionary computation.

2026 · SMILES research project · Alfa-Bank Award

FlowDraft: Flow-Map Drafting for Lossless Parallel Decoding

With Stepan Pavlenko and Yaroslav Pelekhov, I worked on flow-map drafting for lossless parallel decoding. The project received SMILES 2026's Alfa-Bank Award. A public paper is not yet linked here.

Project announcement ↗
2025 · Preprint

On Approaches to Building Surrogate ODE Models for Diffusion Bridges

Interpretable models of diffusion dynamics. I co-led this work and focused on SINDy-based flow matching: learning symbolic differential equations using sparse regression.

Read the paper ↗
2025 · Thesis

Evolutionary NAS for Convolutional KANs

A tailored neural architecture search algorithm and new convolutional KAN layers. My bachelor's thesis, awarded Best Diploma at ITMO and presented at MathAI '25.

Read the paper ↗
2025 · SMILES School Projects Proceedings

Categorical Iterative Proportional Fitting via Discrete Diffusion Models

Co-developed C-IPF, a discrete-diffusion approach to Schrödinger bridges between categorical distributions. Published in the SMILES School Projects Proceedings 2025.

Paper (PDF) ↗
2024 · Q1 Journal · Knowledge-Based Systems

Integration of evolutionary automated machine learning with structural sensitivity analysis for composite pipelines

Published in Knowledge-Based Systems. I contributed to the concept, experiments and manuscript on sensitivity analysis for evolutionary AutoML pipelines.
